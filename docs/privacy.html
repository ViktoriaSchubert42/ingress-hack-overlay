Hack Timer Overlay for Ingress
by GoofyGecko (Viktoria Schubert)
Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Hack Timer Overlay for Ingress does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet access.

What the app stores

The app saves your settings only on your own phone:

  • your chosen faction, button color, size and arrangement
  • your list of timers and counters, their portal mod setup, and when each running timer ends

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android's settings.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionUse
Display over other appsShows the timer buttons on top of Ingress.
Usage accessChecks which app is currently in front, so the buttons only show while Ingress is open. The app only checks whether that app is Ingress. It does not record or keep this information.
NotificationsShows timer countdowns and "timer ended" alerts.
Alarms & remindersSends alerts at the right time while the phone sleeps.
Foreground serviceKeeps the overlay running while you play.
VibrationVibrates when a timer ends.
Run at startupSets your running timers again after the phone restarts.

Internet, analytics and ads

The app does not request internet access. It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party tracking code.
